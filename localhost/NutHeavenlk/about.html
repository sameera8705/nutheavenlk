Nut Heaven.lk is a premium cashew brand concept created to bring together carefully prepared cashews and a sophisticated customer experience.

From classic raw and salted varieties to bold devilled, garlic and roasted flavours, our collection is designed for everyday enjoyment, gifting and sharing.

We believe premium food should feel premium at every touchpoint — from the first impression of the packaging to the moment you open the pack.
